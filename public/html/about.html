About Us

About flavorwell

Flavorwell is a digital platform designed to transform the way people discover, prepare, and enjoy their everyday meals. Combining modern technology with an intuitive cooking experience, Flavorwell offers a diverse library of recipes organized by dish type, dietary preferences, and preparation time, all in a single app.

Mision

Our mission is to democratize access to delicious, healthy, and personalized food through a practical, accessible, and user-friendly digital solution for everyone, from cooking novices to more experienced cooks.

Approach and values

Flavorwell promotes
  • Access to quality food regardless of culinary experience or time constraints.
  • Inclusive options, such as vegan, vegetarian, or low-calorie recipes.
  • Continuously updated, with new recipes and improvements based on user feedback.
  • Sustainable and scalable development, following good programming and web architecture practices.

Who's behind it?

Flavorwell is a project developed by Agustin Mora, a computer systems engineer with experience in full-stack web development. This project represents the fusion of two passions: technology and gastronomy, and seeks to continue evolving as a useful solution with a real impact on users' lives.
